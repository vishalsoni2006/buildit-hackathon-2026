Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything you need to know about eligibility, travel grants, hardware labs, and team formations.

BuildIt 2026 is an All-India collegiate hackathon exclusively open to undergraduate & postgraduate students, engineering scholars, and student developers residing in India. Students from all Indian universities and institutes (IITs, NITs, IIITs, VITs, BITS, and state institutions across all states) are eligible to register!
100% free! Thanks to Mozilla Firefox Club and our university sponsors, there are zero participation fees. In-person hackers at VIT Bhopal receive continuous meals, midnight chai, energy snacks, unlimited coffee, and an exclusive BuildIt kit with official certificates.
Teams can consist of 1 to 4 Indian hackers. Solo participants are eligible for all track awards as well as our dedicated Best Solo Hacker award. If you want teammates, use our built-in Team Matcher board or join our Day 1 formation mixer!
You do! 100% of all intellectual property, code, designs, and startup potential remain solely with you and your team. Neither the organizing club nor the sponsors claim any ownership rights over your creation.
Our hardware loaner depot includes Apple Vision Pro headsets, Meta Quest 3, Raspberry Pi 5 kits, NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano modules, Arduino sensor kits, logic analyzers, and soldering stations staffed by hardware mentors.
Yes! Travel assistance reimbursements (train/bus fares) are provided for selected outstation Indian finalists traveling to the VIT Bhopal Central Campus, sponsored by our organizing committee.
